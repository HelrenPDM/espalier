---
title: Judging and releasing a draft
position: 2
---

:::text{provenance=invented}
A generated draft is ready for release when every statement that a reader could act on has been checked. Comparing two drafts of the same text shows which statements these are.
:::

:::case_comparison{provenance=invented}
**Draft A**

The new filing process saves 40 percent of the working time and has been approved by all departments.

**Draft B**

The new filing process is meant to save working time. The project team will report the measured effect after three months.

Draft A states a figure and an approval. Readers would rely on both, so each needs a check against a source before the release. Draft B states a goal and a plan, and both can be confirmed with the project team.
:::

:::explanation{provenance=invented}
A [[term:hallucination]] rarely looks different from a correct sentence. You therefore judge a draft by the statements that a reader would act on, and you give each of them a [[term:source-check|source check]].
:::

:::callout{provenance=invented}
The release checklist ends with your initials. They record who checked the draft before it was released.
:::
